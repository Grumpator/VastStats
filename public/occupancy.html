How to read this dashboard

Fill = confirmed time. Green is confirmed running time, amber is confirmed idle time and grey is unknown time. Sectors fill clockwise from noon and use the elapsed part of the selected period; future time is excluded. A half-green circle confirms running for half that elapsed time. Occupancy percentages in the tooltip, device rows and summary use running time divided by observed time. Missing telemetry is excluded from that percentage and remains grey in the circle. Read occupancy alongside data coverage. These measure box-time with any rental running, not the fraction of GPUs in use.

Number = runs during the selected period. A stop and restart counts as two. A continuous run counts once in each circle it overlaps.

Border = recorded box state. Green means listed, grey unlisted, amber maintenance and red offline. Borders reflect recorded snapshots, not inferred durations. Tooltips show when offline, maintenance or listing states were recorded; scheduled maintenance is labelled separately. Missing status history is omitted.

Before tracking. A dash means the period predates available history and is excluded from coverage. The first partly tracked circle counts only time from that boundary. This is an archive boundary, not a confirmed first boot. Later gaps remain grey and count as missing.

Markers. A ring marks the current period. An amber dot marks any coverage below 100.0% in the elapsed portion. Fully grey circles have no data; future circles are excluded.

Device icons = latest snapshot. The Vast badge before each device name shows current verification. After the name: one primary state (offline first, otherwise running with instance count, listed, or online); spanner for maintenance; layers for stored count; chip for GPU count; gauge for Vast reliability. Hover the primary icon or tap the device name for snapshot information. Tap a device name to open all details, including timestamps and coverage. GPU and stored-instance icons are hidden on mobile but remain in these details.

Hover or focus a circle for details. Click a month to open its daily view, then a day to open its hourly view. Hourly circles keep tap-to-show details.

Timeline. Each device occupies one row. 24h shows 24 hourly circles including the current hour. Week shows the Monday–Sunday calendar week. Month shows a calendar month in days; Year shows a calendar year in months. Future time never contributes to statistics. Click Device, State or Occupancy to sort; click again to reverse. Missing values stay last. State sorts by offline, running, listed, then online. The arrows move by 24 hours, one calendar week, one calendar month or one calendar year. Forward navigation stops at the current period. Occupancy, Hours and Data coverage refer to the whole selected period, including circles off-screen. Scroll the timeline horizontally on smaller screens; device columns stay fixed. Monthly counts use runs already active at the boundary plus new starts during the period; stop/start episodes count separately.

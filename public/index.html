Hover or focus a square for details. Select a device to open its occupancy history. Bars mean running; flashing means offline. Maintenance takes colour priority over listing. A small clock marks an overdue snapshot; it keeps its last known colour and stops all animation. Unknown states use a neutral outline and question mark. Squares resize to fit the available space, between 22 and 64 pixels. Large estates scroll when the minimum size no longer fits.
